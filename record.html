College football

Does it work? Two FBS seasons replayed a game at a time, each game predicted only from games already played:

anytime touchdownbias −1.4pp, Brier 0.1743 against 0.1921 · top 20% scored 47.3%, bottom 20% 9.5%
receiving yardsbias −1.9pp, Brier 0.2288 against 0.2493 · pool at his level
rushing yardsbias +0.5pp, Brier 0.2297 against 0.2500 · opponent at half strength · pool at his level
rush + rec yardsbias −0.1pp, Brier 0.2280 against 0.2498 · pool at his level
passing yardsbias −1.5pp, Brier 0.1776 against 0.2422 · timid at both ends · opponent at half strength
receptionsbias −0.9pp, Brier 0.2136 against 0.2451
spread51.7% against the closing line — needs 52.4%; inside the noise
total53.4% against the closing line — inside the noise

The touchdown model separates hard and runs a little cold. The receiving yards model runs two points cold, and its replay depends on which games go into the comparison pool by three or four points, so treat it as approximate until the live record below has thousands of rows; rushing yards and receptions are within a point, and passing yards is the one to hold loosely. The game lines are closer to break-even than the NFL's, which is what a softer market looks like, and 1,488 games cannot tell break-even from an edge. Re-check with node backtest-nfl.mjs --league cfb.

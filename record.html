Every number the boards print, graded two ways. The replay runs the model over past seasons a game at a time, each game predicted only from games already played. The live record grades what each board actually published before kickoff, once the games are final. The replay says whether the method works; the live record says whether this board does.

NFL

Does it work? 2023 to 2026 so far, replayed a game at a time, each game predicted only from games already played:

anytime touchdownbias −0.5pp, Brier 0.1557 against 0.1697 · top 20% scored 39.8%, bottom 20% 8.8%
receiving yardsbias −0.1pp, Brier 0.2190 against 0.2440 · pool at his level
rushing yardsbias +2.1pp, Brier 0.2175 against 0.2476 · opponent at half strength · last three games weighted · pool at his level
rush + rec yardsbias −0.1pp, Brier 0.2136 against 0.2451 · pool at his level
passing yardsbias −0.9pp, Brier 0.1637 against 0.2495 · last three games weighted · opponent at half strength
receptionsbias −0.4pp, Brier 0.2008 against 0.2341 · last three games weighted · seasons 4pp apart
spread48.1% against the closing line — under break-even
total52.5% against the closing line — inside the noise

The player props are calibrated on the whole and worth reading; every prop moves a few points between seasons, so hold a single week loosely. The game lines are not: the NFL closing number is very good, and two seasons is not enough games to prove otherwise either way. Re-check with node backtest-nfl.mjs.

NBA

Does it work? A season and a month replayed a night at a time, each night predicted only from games already played, on games no constant was chosen on. Each player is graded at five lines around his projection:

pointsbias −0.1pp, Brier 0.1881 against 0.2479 · 19,620 player-games · worst band 2.1pp
reboundsbias −0.2pp, Brier 0.1939 against 0.2468 · 19,378 player-games · worst band 2.3pp
assistsbias −0.6pp, Brier 0.2027 against 0.2469 · 15,305 player-games · worst band 2.8pp
threes madebias −0.7pp, Brier 0.2171 against 0.2455 · 17,949 player-games · worst band 2.1pp
pts + reb + astbias −0.1pp, Brier 0.1657 against 0.2486 · 19,058 player-games · worst band 3.5pp
every alternate linebias between −0.2pp and +0.1pp by prop over 1,028,801 rungs · no rung off by more than 2.4pp

All five are calibrated to within a point on games the model had not seen, and a little timid at both ends: a chance it calls 83% hit 85%, one it calls 8% hit 6%. Threes are the hardest to call and beat the base rate by the least. Three things this replay cannot see: a player it graded is one who played, so a late scratch is a void bet and not a miss; a teammate ruled out moves nobody's number yet (no dated injury history to measure it on); and nothing here is compared with a book's line, so calibrated is not the same as profitable. Re-check with node backtest-nba.mjs --from 2026 --ladder.

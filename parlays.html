A parlay of lines the book sells as a number and up: 20+ receiving yards, 4+ receptions, 50+ rushing yards, an anytime touchdown. Never an over/under. For each player and stat the leg is the highest line the model gives at least the floor you pick; beside it, how many of his last ten games cleared it, whether the opponent is a soft spot on the cheat sheet for that stat, and what the record did at that line. The slip is the legs most likely to cash under your filters, ranked by chance, never by a price: the site shows chances, not odds. Press I bet this parlay to record it in the bet log, where each leg grades off the box score.
